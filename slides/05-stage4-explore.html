STAGE 4 · THE HEART

Explore ⇄ record findings

Explore with the scientist while every substantive insight is captured automatically as a durable, provenance-pinned finding — a self-updating lab notebook.

The loop

  1. Spark discussion with vetted boilerplate — differential abundance, classifiers, regression, Boruta, enrichment (seeded from lib/ templates).
  2. Go back and forth — held to the conventions: effect size + CI + named correction, no leakage, label-shuffle null, small-n = exploratory.
  3. Findings record themselves — the findings-manager captures every substantive insight automatically, the moment it emerges. You never stop to write anything up; exploration flow is never broken.
  4. Every finding is regenerable — each pins its data version, script + commit, params, environment & seed, with figures embedded inline. Full provenance, by default.
A self-updating lab notebook The durable output is a curated, provenance-tracked findings graph — not a chat transcript. Capture is automatic and generous; the ruthless gate waits for Stage 5.
