STAGE 4 · THE HEART

Explore ⇄ record findings

Explore the data with the scientist and capture every substantive insight as a durable finding — the moment it emerges.

The loop

  1. Spark discussion with vetted boilerplate — differential abundance, classifiers, regression, Boruta, enrichment, dimensionality reduction (seeded from lib/ templates).
  2. Go back and forth — investigate, held to the conventions: effect size + CI + named correction, no leakage, CV matched to the target, label-shuffle null, small-n = exploratory.
  3. Record every insight as a finding — biased toward capturing too much; candidates are cheap. Figures embedded inline with provenance.
  4. Keep the exploration log honest — the multiplicity context behind each finding's caveats.
Skepticism: generous here Capture freely as candidates; the ruthless gate is Stage 5. CPU-heavy results are cached — compute once, re-render figures.
