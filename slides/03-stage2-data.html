STAGE 2

Understand the data

Verify the sample × feature matrix itself — its structure and semantics — before any loader is trusted.

What happens

  1. Orientation, shape, identifiers — determined, not assumed (are rows samples or features?).
  2. Transformation state — log vs linear, checked from the distributions; becomes the scale the loader records.
  3. Missing-value encoding — 0, NA, NaN, "Filtered" are not interchangeable; decide deliberately.
  4. Characterize missingness, contaminants, decoys, duplicates; watch the domain-fidelity traps (gene symbols → dates, scale confusion).
Confirm with the scientist The recorded preprocessing decisions — normalization method (median default; MAD / VSN), batch axis + any confound, and missing-value handling.
