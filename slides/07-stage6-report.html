STAGE 6

Reporting

A report is a projection of the findings graph into prose — not a fresh act of writing.

What happens

  1. Pick the mode — qc (exhaustive, descriptive) or research (selective, narrative — the primary deliverable).
  2. Select + outline the findings — a human checkpoint; the scientist decides.
  3. Write per section from a shared spec; the report-reviewer runs the claim-source + coherence pass.
  4. Assemble under reports/ — abstract written last; optional PDF via pandoc.
Integrity rules Every Results / Discussion sentence maps to a finding id; status + caveats propagate; no invented references.
