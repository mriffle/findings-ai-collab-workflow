How the Findings Workflow answers

Confidence & provenance, by design

You explore the science with an AI collaborator; the workflow does the rest — it keeps the ground solid, and it remembers exactly how every result was made. So nothing rests on trust or memory.

Solid ground

Confidence

The workflow won't build on a shaky foundation.

  • Understand first — no analysis begins until you and the workflow agree on what the data are.
  • It checks the data against itself and against the metadata, and stops if something doesn't add up.
  • Analyses use vetted, standard methods — and every one is independently double-checked.
  • The results that matter are re-derived from scratch before they count as solid.

Nothing gets lost

Provenance

A lab notebook that writes itself.

  • Every substantive insight is captured the moment it appears — automatically.
  • Each finding keeps its evidence, its data, and the exact steps that made it.
  • Every figure carries the data and the recipe behind it.
  • Reports and methods are drawn straight from that record — faithful, not reconstructed.
So every finding — from the first candidate — can be trusted, reproduced, and reported.
