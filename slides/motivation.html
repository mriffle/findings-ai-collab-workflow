Why the Findings Workflow

Confidence & provenance

Two doubts shadow every data analysis — with or without AI. Am I sure I understand my data and analyzed it correctly? And when an insight appears, can I retrace exactly how I got there?

Am I sure?

Confidence

Everything downstream depends on this — one silent mistake and the rest is built on sand.

  • Are my metadata internally consistent — and is it really what I think it is?
  • Do the data have the shape I expect?
  • Do the samples map to their metadata correctly?
  • Am I using an appropriate statistical test — and applying it correctly?

How did I get here?

Provenance

Insight is fragile in the moment — and a result is only as good as the trail behind it.

  • An insight appears mid-analysis — and is gone before it's written down.
  • And if you did record it — what made you believe it?
  • Exactly what data, run through which script, produced this number?
  • How was this figure made — and what data are in it?
Get either wrong, and the finding — however exciting — can't be trusted, reproduced, or reported.
